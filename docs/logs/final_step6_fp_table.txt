# tested, not only the exec rule.

# |---------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------|

# | tainted sh: "/safe/echo hi > report.txt" | refused, report.txt unchanged      | policy: write-down          |
# | tainted sh: "cat /inbox.txt | wc"     | works (cwd /safe)                     | works                       |
